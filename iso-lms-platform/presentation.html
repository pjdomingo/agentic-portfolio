The Engine

Upload a PDF → get a reviewable course

The AI pipeline is the maintainability play: a 20-course catalog costs ~$100–200 in API fees plus expert review time — versus $3k–10k per course with traditional instructional design. Nothing publishes without a human expert signing off.

1Upload PDFStandard guides, policies, audit checklists
2AI outlineModules, lessons & objectives — instructor approves first
3AI lessonsInteractive blocks: text, scenarios, flashcards, quizzes
4AI examsQuestion banks with source references per question
5Expert reviewEdit, regenerate or reject — the quality gate
6PublishVersioned courses, verifiable certificates

Every generated lesson and exam question cites the source passage it came from, so the reviewer verifies against the document — the answer to “can you trust AI with compliance content?”

Business Model

Two stages, one architecture

1

Launch — sell the content

We build the ISO/SOC catalog with the AI pipeline and sell subscriptions to companies preparing for audits.

  • Personal, Group & Enterprise tiers
  • Self-serve via Stripe Checkout — revenue doesn’t wait on sales calls
  • Enterprise wedge: custom courses generated from the client’s own policies
2

Later — sell the platform

White-label the proven engine to companies and schools that want their own branded LMS.

  • Tenant licensing ~$500–1,500/mo per organization
  • Multi-tenant from day 1 (org-scoped data, per-tenant branding) — a packaging change, not a rewrite
  • Gated on Stage 1 traction (~20+ paying orgs)
